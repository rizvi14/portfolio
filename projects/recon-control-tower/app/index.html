How the matcher scores

Breaks are injected at known rates with labels, and the pipeline is graded against them by the one model allowed to see the answers. Precision is whether the queue can be trusted; recall is whether money can leave unnoticed.

Recall by injected break type

Transaction grain. Non-breaks (in transit, partial refunds, NOC renames) are graded on not being raised — every one of them scores clean.

What each rail actually does

The contractual window is a policy number. The investigation threshold is calibrated from the observed p99 arrival lag of cleanly matched pairs, floored at policy + grace and capped so a degrading rail cannot widen its own definition of on-time.
